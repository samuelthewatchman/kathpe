❤️ High Blood Pressure

Add blood pressure picture here

What is it?

Blood pressure is the force of your blood pushing against the walls of your blood tubes, called blood vessels.

When the push is too strong all the time, we say you have high blood pressure. It is also called hypertension.

How you feel

Many people feel nothing at first. Sometimes you may feel:

  • 🤕 Headache
  • 😵‍💫 Feeling dizzy (the room spins)
  • 😮‍💨 Feeling very tired
  • 💔 Pain in the chest
  • Feeling short of breath

What to do

  • 🏥 Go to the hospital so the doctor can check your blood pressure
  • 💊 The doctor may give you medicine to make the pressure lower
  • 🧂 Eat less salt
  • 🥦 Eat more fruits and vegetables
  • 🚶 Walk or exercise most days
  • 🚭 Do not smoke or drink too much alcohol

How to keep it under control

  • Check your blood pressure often
  • Take your medicine every day if the doctor gives it
  • Keep a healthy weight
  • 😴 Rest and sleep well
