🧴 Skin Infections

Add skin infection picture here

What is it?

Skin infections happen when germs — like fungus, bacteria, or tiny mites — get into the skin and cause problems like rashes, itching, or sores.

Common ones include ringworm (a fungus, not a real worm), scabies (tiny itchy mites), and boils (painful bumps from bacteria).

How you feel

  • 🔴 Red, itchy patches on the skin
  • Rings or circles on the skin (common in ringworm)
  • Painful bumps or boils
  • Scratching a lot, especially at night
  • Sores that may leak fluid

What to do

  • 🏥 Go to the hospital or clinic if it does not get better, spreads, or looks infected
  • 💊 The doctor may give cream or medicine depending on the cause
  • Do not scratch too much — it can make it worse or spread it
  • Keep the area clean and dry

How to prevent it

  • 🧼 Wash your body regularly with soap and clean water
  • 👕 Wear clean clothes and change them often
  • Do not share towels, combs, or clothes with someone who has a skin infection
  • Keep your surroundings clean
