ℹ️ About This Website

This website is called KATH P.E.

It means Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital Patient Education.

It was made to help patients and families learn about common sicknesses in very simple words.

Important Notice

This website is only for learning. It is not a substitute for seeing a real doctor.

If you are sick, please go to Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital or the nearest clinic. 🏥

Always follow the advice of your doctor or nurse.

Where Our Information Comes From

All health information on this site is based on guidance from the World Health Organization (WHO) and the Ghana Health Service (GHS).

Each topic page lists its specific sources at the bottom, along with the name of the health worker who reviewed it for accuracy.
