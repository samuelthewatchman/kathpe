🩸 Diabetes (Sugar Disease)

Add diabetes picture here

What is it?

Diabetes is a sickness where there is too much sugar in your blood.

Your body needs a helper called insulin. Insulin is like a key that opens the door so sugar can go into your body cells and give you energy.

When there is not enough insulin, or it does not work well, sugar stays in the blood and makes you sick.

How you feel

  • 🥤 You feel very thirsty
  • 🚻 You urinate (pee) many times
  • 😮‍💨 You feel tired and weak
  • 🍽️ You feel hungry all the time
  • Cuts and wounds heal slowly
  • You may lose weight without trying

What to do

  • 🏥 Go to the hospital
  • The doctor will check the sugar in your blood
  • 💉 You may need medicine or insulin injections
  • 🥗 Eat healthy food at the right times
  • 🍬 Do not eat too many sweet things

How to live well

  • Take your medicine every day
  • Eat vegetables, fruits, and whole foods
  • 🚶 Walk or exercise every day
  • Check your blood sugar as the doctor tells you
  • 🦶 Take care of your feet — wash them and look for sores
