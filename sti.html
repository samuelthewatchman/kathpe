🛡️ STIs (Special Body Germs)

Add picture here

What are STIs?

STIs are special germs that can pass from one person to another. They pass when the private parts of two people touch during close adult body contact.

These germs can make the private parts, and sometimes other parts of the body, sick.

How you may feel

  • Pain when you urinate (pee)
  • Strange liquid coming from the private parts
  • Sores or bumps on the private parts
  • Pain in the lower stomach
  • Itching in the private parts
  • Some people feel nothing at first

What to do

  • 🏥 Go to the hospital or clinic as soon as possible
  • 💊 The doctor can give medicine to kill the germs
  • Tell the doctor everything so they can help you properly
  • Both partners should get treated at the same time

How to protect yourself

  • The safest way is not to have close body contact until you are ready and protected
  • If adults choose to have close contact, use a condom every time
  • Go for check-ups if you think you may have an STI

Important: STIs can be serious if not treated. Always see a doctor. Do not try to treat it by yourself.
