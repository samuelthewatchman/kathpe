🧼 Hand Washing

Add hand washing picture here

Why wash your hands?

Your hands pick up tiny germs — very small living things that can make you sick.

When you touch your eyes, nose, or mouth, the germs can go inside your body. Washing hands with soap kills many germs and keeps you healthy.

When to wash

  • 🍽️ Before you eat
  • 🚻 After you use the toilet
  • 🤧 After you sneeze or cough
  • After you touch dirty things or play outside
  • 👶 Before you touch a baby
  • 🐾 After you touch animals

How to wash the right way

  • 💧 Wet your hands with clean water
  • 🧼 Put soap on your hands
  • Rub for 20 seconds (sing Happy Birthday two times!)
  • Rub between fingers and under nails
  • Rinse and dry with a clean cloth

No soap and water?

Use hand sanitizer — the clear gel that kills germs. Rub it on your hands until they are dry. ✨
