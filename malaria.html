🦟 Malaria

Add malaria picture here

What is malaria?

Malaria is a sickness.

It is caused by a very tiny living thing called a parasite.

A parasite is a small creature that lives inside another living thing and makes it sick.

This parasite lives in the blood.

How does a person get malaria?

Only a special kind of mosquito can give you malaria.

It is the female mosquito.

Male mosquitoes do not bite people for blood.

Female mosquitoes bite people because they need blood to make their eggs.

When a female mosquito bites a person who already has malaria, she sucks up the parasite with the blood.

Later, when the same mosquito bites a healthy person, she puts the parasite into that person’s blood.

That is how malaria spreads from one person to another.

How you feel

  • Your body feels very hot (this is called fever)
  • You feel cold and your body shakes
  • Your head hurts a lot
  • Your body feels weak and tired
  • You may vomit (throw up)
  • You may have pain in your body or tummy
  • Sometimes young children can become very sleepy or have fits

What to do

  • Go to the hospital or clinic quickly
  • The doctor will take a little blood to check for the parasite
  • The doctor will give you special medicine that kills the parasite
  • Drink lots of clean water
  • Rest in bed
  • Take all the medicine even if you start to feel better

How to stop malaria

  • Sleep under a mosquito net every night
  • The net stops the female mosquito from biting you
  • Do not leave standing water (water that stays still) near the house
  • Female mosquitoes lay their eggs in standing water
  • Keep grass short and the area clean
  • Wear long clothes in the evening
