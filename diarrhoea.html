💧 Diarrhoea

Add diarrhoea picture here

What is it?

Diarrhoea is when you pass watery poop many times in a day.

It usually happens because of germs in dirty food or water, but it can also come from other sicknesses.

How you feel

  • 💧 Watery, runny poop, often many times a day
  • Stomach pain or cramps
  • 🤢 Sometimes vomiting
  • 😮‍💨 Feeling weak or tired
  • A dry mouth and feeling very thirsty — this means the body is losing too much water (called dehydration)

What to do

  • 💧 Drink lots of clean water and oral rehydration salts (ORS) — a special mix that helps replace lost water and salts
  • 🏥 Go to the hospital, especially for babies, young children, or if it does not stop after a day or two
  • Keep eating small, simple meals if you can
  • Go quickly if you see blood in the poop, or the sick person becomes very weak or confused

How to prevent it

  • 💧 Drink only clean or boiled water
  • 🧼 Wash hands with soap before eating and after using the toilet
  • 🍽️ Cook food well and keep it covered from flies
  • Wash fruits and vegetables before eating
