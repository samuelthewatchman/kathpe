💪 Anaemia (Low Blood)

Add anaemia picture here

What is it?

Anaemia means you do not have enough healthy red blood cells.

Red blood cells carry oxygen (the air we need) to every part of your body. When you have too few, your body doesn't get enough oxygen — and that makes you feel weak.

How you feel

  • 😮‍💨 You feel very tired and weak
  • Your skin or eyes may look pale (light in colour)
  • You feel short of breath
  • 💓 Your heart may beat fast
  • 😵‍💫 You may feel dizzy or get headaches

What to do

  • 🏥 Go to the hospital
  • The doctor will check your blood
  • 💊 You may need iron medicine or special food
  • 🍊 Eat foods that help make blood: meat, fish, eggs, beans, leafy greens, and fruits like oranges

How to prevent it

  • Eat foods rich in iron every day
  • 🤰 If you are pregnant, go for regular check-ups
  • Treat malaria quickly — it can cause anaemia
