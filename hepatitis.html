🟡 Hepatitis B

Add hepatitis picture here

What is it?

Hepatitis B is a sickness caused by a virus that attacks the liver — a body part that cleans your blood and helps digest food.

It spreads through blood or body fluids — for example from an infected mother to her baby at birth, sharing sharp objects like blades or needles, or close adult body contact.

How you feel

  • Many people feel nothing for a long time, which is why testing matters
  • 😮‍💨 Feeling very tired
  • 🤢 Feeling sick in the stomach, or vomiting
  • Pain in the stomach area
  • 🟡 Yellow eyes or yellow skin (this is called jaundice)
  • Dark-coloured urine (pee)

What to do

  • 🏥 Go to the hospital to get tested — a simple blood test can check for hepatitis B
  • The doctor will guide treatment and monitoring based on your results
  • Avoid alcohol, which can further stress the liver
  • Family members should also get tested and vaccinated if needed

How to prevent it

  • 💉 The hepatitis B vaccine is the best protection, and is given to babies at birth in Ghana
  • Do not share blades, needles, or other sharp personal items
  • Make sure any injections or piercings use new, sterile equipment
  • Pregnant women should get tested so their babies can be protected at birth
