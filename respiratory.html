🫁 Cough & Chest Infections

Add respiratory picture here

What is it?

This covers common sicknesses that make it hard to breathe or make you cough, like colds, chest infections, and pneumonia.

Pneumonia is a serious infection inside the lungs — the body parts that help you breathe.

These sicknesses can come from germs called viruses or bacteria, often spread through coughing, sneezing, or dirty air.

How you feel

  • 😷 Coughing, sometimes with thick mucus
  • 😮‍💨 Fast or difficult breathing
  • 🔥 Fever
  • 💔 Chest pain, especially when breathing or coughing
  • Feeling weak or tired
  • In babies and small children, fast breathing or the chest pulling in is a danger sign

What to do

  • 🏥 Go to the hospital, especially if breathing is fast, difficult, or the person is very weak
  • 💊 The doctor may give medicine depending on the cause
  • 💧 Drink plenty of fluids
  • 🛌 Rest

How to prevent it

  • 🧼 Wash hands often, especially after coughing or sneezing
  • 🤧 Cover your mouth with your elbow, not your hand, when you cough or sneeze
  • 💉 Make sure children get their vaccines on time
  • 🚭 Avoid smoke — from cigarettes, cooking fires, or dust — where possible
