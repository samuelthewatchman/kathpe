🌡️ Typhoid Fever

Add typhoid picture here

What is it?

Typhoid fever is a sickness caused by bacteria — very tiny germs that are different from the parasite that causes malaria.

You get it from eating food or drinking water that has been dirtied by these germs, often from someone else's waste.

How you feel

  • 🔥 A fever that keeps getting worse over several days
  • 🤕 Headache
  • 😮‍💨 Feeling weak and tired
  • 🤢 Stomach pain, and sometimes vomiting
  • Loss of appetite
  • Some people get a rash of small pink spots

What to do

  • 🏥 Go to the hospital — typhoid needs medicine (antibiotics) from a doctor
  • Do not try to treat it yourself at home
  • 💧 Drink plenty of clean water
  • 🛌 Rest until you feel better

How to prevent it

  • 💧 Drink only clean or boiled water
  • 🍽️ Eat food that is well cooked and served hot
  • 🧼 Wash your hands with soap before eating and after using the toilet
  • Wash fruits and vegetables well before eating
