🫀 Tuberculosis (TB)

Add TB picture here

What is it?

Tuberculosis, or TB, is a serious infection caused by bacteria that mostly attacks the lungs.

It spreads through the air when a person with TB coughs, sneezes, or talks, and someone nearby breathes in the germs.

How you feel

  • 😷 A cough that lasts more than 2 weeks
  • Sometimes coughing up blood
  • 💔 Chest pain
  • 🌙 Night sweats
  • Losing weight without trying
  • 😮‍💨 Feeling weak and tired all the time
  • 🔥 Fever that comes and goes

What to do

  • 🏥 Go to the hospital if you have a cough lasting more than 2 weeks
  • The doctor will test for TB, usually by checking your sputum (the mucus you cough up)
  • 💊 TB is treated with a course of medicine over several months — it is important to take all of it, even after you start feeling better
  • Cover your mouth when coughing to protect others

How to prevent it

  • 💉 Babies can get a vaccine (BCG) that helps protect against severe TB
  • 🪟 Keep rooms well aired out with fresh air
  • If someone in your house has TB, everyone should get checked
  • Finish all TB medicine if you are ever treated — stopping early can make it harder to cure
